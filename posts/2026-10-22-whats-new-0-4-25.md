---
title: 0.4.25: what's new
date: 2026-10-22
---
- **The first Universe Wipe keeps all your ships.** Every other item goes, except the gear on your active ship, your drones, drone formations, P.E.T. Stations and your Transport Cache. Clans end. Credits, Thulium, XP, honor, level, ranks, Skylab, technologies and skins stay.
- **Auction buy orders** (level 5 or more): say what you want and what you pay; the price is held until a seller fills the order or you cancel.
- **A News card** on the Dashboard shows the newest posts of the SpaceCorps team, like this one.
- **Mining:** the Thulium Geode pays **36 to 84** Thulium and the daily mining limit is gone.
- **Davidski** is out of every ranking, hits twice as hard and pays 1.5 times. The x-2 maps hold 30 Seekers and 15 Phantasms.
- The **Mole** works every rock it can reach, the **Cradle** works in flight, and **chat** takes Cyrillic, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.
- **Interface:** drones on the pilot sheet, Ctrl+F search in the Assembly, ship names in the Forge, short numbers (1.2M), a notice when the Skylab Trophies Bay is full, and a clan timer that reads --:-- when the clock is unknown.
