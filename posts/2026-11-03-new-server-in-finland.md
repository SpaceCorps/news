The SpaceCorps universe now runs on a new, dedicated server in Helsinki, Finland, powered by an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X3D. Thank you for your patience during the short downtime while we moved.

## What moved
- The whole universe: every pilot, ship, drone, clan, quest, ranking and the current season (day 27) came across untouched.
- The game address is the same. Nothing to install or change.

## What you will notice
- You were signed out once during the move. Log in again and you are back where you left off.
- Players in and around Europe should see better connection times to the new location.
