---
title: Next wipe: build your Transport Cache
date: 2026-10-30
---
**When**
- The server clock is on **day 23 of 30**. You have **one week** to build your **Transport Cache** in the **Galaxy Gates** menu.
- On **day 27** the black hole starts to grow. At the **end of day 30** it explodes and the server is removed. You move to a new one.

**What you do**
- **Lock in what you carry over** at any moment, and **choose the server you play on next**.
- We suggest **Alpha** for new pilots and **Beta** for experienced pilots.
- Each wipe you get **1 free transport token** to move to another server at any time. An admin can help you move if things go wrong.

**In the Transport Cache**
- **One ship**, locked in with **all its equipment**. Fit it with the best you have: you cannot fly it until the next wipe.
- **10 extra slots** each for lasers, shields and engines.
- **100 tons** of extra space for anything. Every item has a weight.

**Kept without you doing anything**
- A generous amount of **ammo, rockets, Thulium and Credits**.
- **Drones, special items and containers**.
- **Skylab, research progress, Wipe Point progress and P.E.T.s**.

**New this wipe**
- On a fresh wipe you can **redo the missions you completed**. They pay **experience and honor only**.
- If you make **no choice**, the system picks the best guess for you.
- This is the **first run** of the system and things may go wrong. We **compensate anyone affected** and improve it from your feedback and bug reports.
