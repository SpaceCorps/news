---
title: 0.5.10: a harder Nightmare, chat for Premium, emotes and translation
date: 2026-11-20
---

0.5.10 is out on Helsinki, Singapore and US East, and chat translation is on. Update the game, or get it from the [download page](https://play.spacecorps.tech/) or Hangar. Most of this release was shown by tests and models, not by people playing it yet.

**Davidski's Nightmare and the clan dungeons**
- **The Nightmare is much harder.** Every ship of the gate deals 3 times the damage, Evil Davidski has 10 times the hull and shield, and he cannot be hurt at all while any golden ship lives. His rocket barrages fire Dormant Lances. Planned time: about 84 minutes, not 45 to 60.
- **Phantom's Lance has three illusions** and **a clan dungeon pays at 0.1 % of the boss** (it was 0.2 %).
- **A Clan Warden keeps its whole hull and shield** at any crew size. Its damage, mending and reinforcements still follow the crew.
- **The Skylab brings twice the kills** at the same hull wear an hour as before.
- **Personal loot crates for groups,** cut by the damage each pilot dealt. **The Forge:** a tier-up has a 25 % chance of one free buff. **The Dormant Keycard** for whoever claims the 20th Signal.

**Chat**
- **Chat translation is on.** Right-click another pilot's line, or a private message, and choose Translate: that line (at most 200 characters) and the language you read go to our translation service, an AI model on our own computer, or Claude (Anthropic) for Hungarian and when the computer is not available. 20 translations a day, 200 with Premium. Machine translation can be wrong. The [Privacy Policy](https://play.spacecorps.tech/privacy/) says what is sent.
- **Premium in chat:** 105 emoji and one of ten colours for a message, and a gold name for every pilot who has paid for Premium. Typed smileys like :) now go out as emoji.
- **The emote wheel:** hold N and slide to one of sixteen emotes. Ten a day, no limit with Premium.
- **Kick, mute and ban notices** are lines of the Global chat.
- **5 Chroma Archives with every 30-day Premium payment** (cosmetic cases, every prize is a look). Premium does not make ships stronger.

**Also**
- A more readable flight HUD: the numbers on the bars and the phone's buttons read over bright space.
- Registering needs a ticked box: you are 18 or older and accept the [Terms of Use](https://play.spacecorps.tech/terms/) and the Privacy Policy.
- The Android and iOS apps are in closed tests and still works in progress.

**Weaker or costlier**
- A small clan may not be able to beat a Clan Warden any more.
- The Nightmare takes about an hour and a half, and a clan dungeon party can no longer out-damage the Phantom's Lance heal until its illusions are dead.
- The Items and Formations wheels give one place to the emote wheel.

**Servers and your account**
- The server and the game went live together. No Credits, Thulium or items move. A pilot with active paid Premium gets 5 Chroma Archives once at the update, and a pilot who claimed the 20th Signal gets the Dormant Keycard once.

**Known limits**
- On the Helsinki server the emote wheel may say "Emotes are not available on this server" until the next update: a bug we found after release. Singapore and US East show it.
- The first translation after a quiet hour can take a few seconds while the model wakes up; Claude answers meanwhile.
- Nobody has played the harder Nightmare or the three illusions at real size yet.
