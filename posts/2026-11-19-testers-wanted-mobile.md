title: Testers wanted for the Android and iOS apps
date: 2026-11-19
---

We are looking for testers for the SpaceCorps 2027 apps on **Android phones** and on **iPhone and iPad**. Both apps are still works in progress: the touch controls, the screen layouts and the speed on real devices need people who play on real devices.

**How to get in**
- **On Discord:** join [our Discord](https://discord.gg/VjW67tkrTb) and tell us you want to test, with the kind of device you have.
- **By email:** write to mikael@spacecorpstechnology.com with the same.

**What we need from your message**
- **Android:** the Google account email you use in the Play Store, and your phone model. Android testers join through a Google Play test link.
- **iPhone or iPad:** the email of your Apple ID, and your device model. iOS testers join through TestFlight, Apple's test app.

**What to expect**
- You play with your own pilot on the same servers as everybody else. The apps will have bugs, and some screens will be hard to read or to touch. That is what we want to hear about.
- Access comes in steps as Google and Apple approve the test builds, so it can take a few days after you ask. We will write back to you when your invitation is sent.
- Tell us what breaks, what is hard to do with a finger, and what runs slowly. A screenshot helps.

Thank you for helping us get the game onto phones.
