---
title: 0.5.8: alien tiers, new gate counts, a harder dungeon boss
date: 2026-11-17
---

0.5.8 is the alien tiers and Galaxy Gates update. Nobody has flown most of the new numbers yet. Update the game, or get it from the [download page](https://spacecorps.github.io/play/) or Hangar.

**Aliens and gates**
- **Alien tiers.** A Greek letter in front of an alien's name shows its tier: Alpha, Beta (1.5 times as tough, pays 2 times), Gamma (2 times, 3 times) and a new Delta (4 times, 6 times). Swarms, Davidski, the Dormant, Warden waves and dungeon aliens have none.
- **The Materializer asks for a category:** the Chrono-Gate, Alpha-Beta-Gamma or Delta-Omega. New part counts: Chrono-Gate 13, Alpha Rift 14, Beta Breach 33, Gamma Foundry 68, Delta Siege 96, Omega Vault 119. A part of the five solo gates is found two to three times as often, so the parts of a run cost less Thulium in five of the six gates and a little more in the Chrono-Gate.
- **All gates can be built at once,** with a portal for each. You play one at a time.

**Dungeons and combat**
- **A harder clan dungeon boss.** From stage 2 it fires at two pilots at once. The Rocket barrage hits every pilot for 50,000 to 75,000 (more at stages 2 and 3). The Meteor drop is 30 % wider and hits twice as hard. A new ability, Phantom's Lance, brings two illusions that heal it. Clan dungeons pay four times the Echo Shards of 0.5.7, and the five aliens that drop them do so twice as often.
- **Your own rocket blast hurts you** for half of what it does to others, the N.U.K.E. too. **Storm RECON** gets Target and Slow. **A cloak survives a safe zone** and a portal jump.
- **Balance:** the group share radius is 8,000 units, and THUNDER (128,000 hull, was 96,000), BUCKY, DUMA, NOTSUM and BRATAN change.

**Skins and more**
- **Skins:** a Miyuki skin in every collection, and a LOU collection for the clan LOU (given out by an administrator for now).
- **Also:** a new **Ubuntu font** on every system, **Xbox and PlayStation controllers** on a computer, and the **web version** at web.spacecorps.xyz, which runs the game in a browser. The phone controls are in the code only: the Android and iOS apps are not part of this release.
- **Premium** gives a badge and no gameplay advantage. Planned, not available yet and subject to change: emoji support in chat, a reaction wheel and chat translations.

**Weaker or costlier**
- A completion pays 25 % less ammo, and the Chrono-Gate costs a little more Thulium. A gate's waves wear tiers too, and you take about 17 % more damage from them (a model figure).
- The dungeon boss is harder. Your own blast can destroy you. Tesseract Facets hit 1.5 times harder. Premium no longer doubles experience.
- Converting a Paragon to THUNDER now takes 5 Dark Matter Plates (4) and to BUCKY 6 (5).
- A safe zone no longer ends a cloak, so it no longer reveals a cloaked ship.
- Higher tiers appear in every world, so a world's aliens are a little tougher and pay a little more.
- A Chroma Archive needs about 253 openings at the median for the whole pool (214 before): each collection gains a Miyuki skin.
- Clan members can see how many days ago each clanmate played, and a pilot cannot switch it off.

**Servers and your account**
- The server and the game go live together on Helsinki, Singapore and US East. A game from before 0.5.8 cannot spin the Materializer: it is told to update.
- The update moves no Credits, Thulium or items, except your **gate parts,** converted once so that a complete gate stays complete (Chrono-Gate 1 of 2 becomes 6 of 13). Wipe Points for the skill levels you already have are paid once at your first claim.

**Known limits**
- Nobody has played the new gate economy, five built gates at once, the alien tiers on a busy sky, the dungeon boss with a real party or the controllers. The web version was run only in headless Chrome on one PC: it draws Japanese, Korean and Chinese as boxes (a fix is planned for the next patch) and has no controller support.
