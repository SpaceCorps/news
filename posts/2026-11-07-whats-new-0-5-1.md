---

The server already runs 0.5.1. Update the game from Steam, Hangar or the [download page](https://spacecorps.github.io/play/). A 0.5.0 game keeps working but cannot open or enter the new Gates.

**Clan Gates**
- The maze now fills the whole map, with fog. Your party starts together in a shut room.
- The Warlord's room stays locked until the elite guards (Crystalys and their Twins) are dead.
- A **Clan Gate portal** on your home map, a **call card with Join**, and a **window of its own** bring the clan to the gate.
- Gates open from **season day 1** (day 4 before). Longer time limits (**95 / 130 / 165 minutes**), **six** loot zones, boosters from stage 2.
- Every paid pilot now gets **5 / 10 / 15 Orvium Reinforced Plates**, and a gear card gives **every paid pilot** a piece forged for him.

**Beta and Gamma**
- Swarms, giant excavators, the Danger Sectors' rocks and PvP are there from **season day 1**. Alpha keeps its Peace Protocol on days 1 to 3.

**Ranking and your account**
- **The ranking starts from zero for this season:** the Hall of Fame, the ladders, your rank and the crown now count only what you have earned since the wipe on 6 November. Your level, experience, honor and all-time kills are unchanged.
- A mission pays the higher of the world it was done in and the world you fly in.
- Engines and lasers fitted in your Skylab stay at a wipe.
- Stacks of one item that the Transport Cache had split are joined again. Nothing was lost.

**Also**
- Boss Crystalys is twice as tough and gives **30** PvE points. The Tesseract fights with **100** Facets. Ammo crafts without Power Cores or Ship Fragments. The Magic Hammer's 60-day limit is gone. The Steam link works again.
