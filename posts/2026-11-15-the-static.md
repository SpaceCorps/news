title: The Static: twenty Signals
date: 2026-11-15
---

Mission Control is picking up something strange. A series of twenty missions, the **Signals**, comes with 0.5.7. Each one is a small mystery.

**How it works**
- Open Mission Control. If you have come a little way, Signal 1 will be waiting. Claim it and the next one opens. They come one at a time.
- Every Signal you claim shows you **one letter** of a personal 20-letter code.
- Your code is **yours alone**. It is different for every pilot, so a friend's letters or answers will not help you.
- The letters do not arrive in the order of the code. Keep track of what you find and piece it together.
- Claim all twenty, then type your code into the bonus code box on the Thulium page. Something is waiting for the pilots who get there.
- A Signal is never repeated, and a wipe does not take your letters.

**Good to know**
- Not every Signal is a kill count. Expect to look, count, wait and think.
- A few open only later in the season, and at least one needs a clan.
- Please keep the answers to yourselves and the hints friendly. Let people find it for themselves.
