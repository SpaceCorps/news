---
title: 0.5.9: Davidski's Nightmare, the system font is back, rockets for new pilots
date: 2026-11-19
---

0.5.9 brings Davidski's Nightmare, a seventh Galaxy Gate. Most of it was shown by tests and screenshots, not by people playing. Update the game, or get it from the [download page](https://spacecorps.github.io/play/) or Hangar.

**Davidski's Nightmare**
- **A new gate with its own Materializer category.** 238 parts at 13 % a spin, 183,077 Thulium on average without the guarantee: the price of Omega Vault.
- **12 waves of "Evil" ships** of every base hull. They fire rockets and mend themselves. Then **Evil Davidski:** at half his hull he turns ANGRY, plays his song and calls 26 golden ships, one of every ship in the game. While one lives, only a fifth of your damage gets through to him.
- **Davidski mocks you** in a window at every wave. Killing Evil Davidski counts as a kill of Davidski for your quests.
- The planned time is 45 to 60 minutes. His real size was never played: the boss was only tried on a shrunk copy of the data.

**For your ship**
- **Miyuki Channel Shield:** twice the shield points (150,000 a second to the linked ship, 30,000 to you), 80 seconds between uses at level V, and 3, 4 and 5 % of each ship's own maximum shield a second at levels III, IV and V.
- **New pilots start with 100 each of Lancet I, Rivet I, Ember I and Scatter I** (400 rockets). Pilots who already enlisted get none.

**Your own numbers**
- **Ability tooltips** now include your Pilot Skills (Tactics, Healing, Sabotage, Reflex, Cloak Recharge), and "Ready in" counts the seconds you really wait.
- **Quest briefings** name your world's hull allowance and time limit, not Alpha's.

**Also**
- **The font:** the game on a computer draws the system's UI font again, as until 0.5.7. The web version and the phone builds keep Ubuntu Light.
- **The web version on phones and tablets:** a keyboard for the sign-in on Android, the page height on iPad, sound after the first tap and a bigger menu. This was only tried in headless Chrome, never on a real phone.
- **Administrators** can name the tier of the aliens they spawn.
- Chinese, Japanese and Korean now draw in the web version: the page downloads one font file of about 2 MB (1.4 to 1.5 MB on the wire) from our own site when it is needed. The common Traditional Chinese characters draw too. Rare characters still draw as boxes.
- An administrator's broadcast, the kick, mute and ban lines and the Helsinki server's restart warnings (5 minutes down to 30 seconds) now reach pilots on Singapore and US East too.
- The Android and iOS apps are still works in progress.

**Weaker or costlier**
- If you liked the Ubuntu font on a computer, it is gone there and there is no setting for it.
- The Nightmare is as expensive as Omega Vault, 183,077 Thulium on average.

**Servers and your account**
- The server and the game go live together on Helsinki, Singapore and US East. The update moves no Credits, Thulium or items. A game from before 0.5.9 does not show the Nightmare.

**Known limits**
- Nobody has played the Nightmare's boss at his real size, heard his song on speakers or measured the golden escort with real numbers. The working beam of the Evil ships' repair drones was only seen in scripted scenes, never on a real server ship. A touch phone has not run the web fixes. The new System lines on Singapore and US East were tested with a real Helsinki server and two regional servers in one process, not on the live servers. The Chinese, Japanese and Korean font was checked in headless Chrome on one PC only.
