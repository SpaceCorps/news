**The Universe Wipe is rebuilt**
- It keeps **every ship**, your drones, drone formations, P.E.T.s and P.E.T. Stations.
- **Transport Cache:** one locked ship that keeps all its gear, three bays of 10 (lasers, shield cores, engines) and 100 t of cargo (was 50 t). It opens when the Chrono-Gate is built.
- Up to 1,000 of each combat rocket and 1,000,000 of each laser ammo type carry over by themselves.
- Every other item goes, and clans end.
- Credits above 5 billion and Thulium above 10 million are lost at a capped wipe (the rules are in the earlier post on the wipe changes).
- A **Wipe preview** shows what the wipe will do to you, and a **Season Report** shows what you kept and were paid.

**The wipe pays by rank**
- 5 to 400 Wipe Points, up to 7 Premium days, Credits, Thulium, loot containers and a season title.
- The top ranks also get a look.

**New**
- **Five loot containers:** Specimen Vault, Foundry Crucible, Chroma Archive, Survey Canister and Reagent Rack. They are earned, never sold, and the odds are published.
- **40 achievements and 32 titles** (Community › Achievements).
- **Daily rewards:** a calendar of 29 tiles with four chests.
- **Six special items**, issued by administrators for now.
- **Stats** for This wipe, Total and Past wipes.

**Smaller**
- Beta needs level 7 and Gamma level 10.
- The black hole grows up to 1.8 times in the last 72 hours before the wipe.
- The Galaxy Gates spin for Thulium only.
- P.E.T. Station II is made from a Station I, and III from a II.
- Davidski's Auction lots come last.
- New sounds for the new screens, and the wiki is updated in 13 languages.
