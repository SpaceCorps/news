---
title: 0.5.0: the PvE update, what's new
date: 2026-11-04
---

0.5.0 is the **PvE update**: clans open Gates, boss swarms and the Tesseract roam the Danger Sectors, and laser ammo gets a research tree.

**New**
- **Clan Gates:** a co-leader or the leader opens a Gate from the clan bank (**500,000 to 2,000,000 Credits**). **4 to 10 clan members** cross a maze, hold **4 loot zones** and bring down the **Warlord**. Stage 3 pays each pilot **100,000 Honor, 17,500,000 Credits, 15,000 Thulium and 15,000 x4 ammo**, once a day and at most **10 paid clears a week**. See the new **Gates** tab of the clan window (from season day 4).
- **The Tesseract:** a mothership in Danger Sector 4 with **1,600,000 hull and 1,200,000 shield** in Alpha. It mends **125,000 a second** when left alone and calls up to **50 Facets** while it is hit. A fight for a very large group.
- **Three boss swarms:** a Boss Phantasm and a Boss Goombah on `x-3` and `x-4`, and a Boss Crystalys in `DS-1` to `DS-3`, each with guards that heal it.
- **Laser ammo research:** **Military Grade Ammo I and II** open the crafting of x3 and x4 (the x4 is no longer sold). New ammo: **Siphon Battery II**, **Antimatter Battery I and II** (they pierce 20 %) and **Dark Matter Ammo I and II** (**x1.5 to x5** a volley, **x6** with a shield leech).
- **Every alien has its own shot** and its own sound: plasma blobs, spores, crystal shards, slugs, ion orbs and slag, with **32 new sounds**. Your lasers are unchanged.
- **Missions come back after a wipe:** every mission you completed, the 50 Challenge missions too, opens for one **Repeat** a season that pays **in full** (Credits, Thulium and items too, not only experience and honor as the 30 October note said).
- **Skylab:** Solar keeps **100 %** of its power while it upgrades, and a running upgrade can be **cancelled for a full refund**.

**Changed**
- **Balance:** Field Theory **+4 / +7 / +10**, Basic and Heavy Shield Core **50 / 55 %**, Engine II and III **3 / 4 %**, Adaptive Cores at half, the Afterburner **+20 / 30 / 40 % for 15 s**.
- **Clan Wardens:** every wave is now a mix of Phantasm, Bulwarks and Goombahs, and pays more.
- **Danger Sectors:** no respawn on the spot. You come back at your base or the nearest portal.
- **Giant excavator:** a cloaked ship cannot use it.
- **Daily gifts:** ammo and tier III rockets only, and the calendar is worth about **50 %** more.
- **Smaller:** the Thulium Geode pays **72 to 168** Thulium, Moles can share an asteroid, special items are tiles in the Hangar inventory, and repair slots show their healing.
- **Wiki:** new pages for the Gates and the boss swarms, in 13 languages.

**To get it**
- **Update the game** to see the new screens. The server and the new game go live together, and the update moves no Credits, Thulium or items.
- A 0.4.27 game keeps working, but it cannot open or enter a Gate or pick the new ammo.
- **The next wipe** is on Friday 6 November, just after 00:00 UTC. Your Repeats open at that wipe.
- The full notes are on the [download page](https://spacecorps.github.io/play/).
