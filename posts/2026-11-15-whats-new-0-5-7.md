---
title: 0.5.7: Forge Sockets, the Miyuki and new missions
date: 2026-11-15
---

0.5.7 is a very big patch that is out now, and nobody has flown most of the new numbers yet: they come from paper and tests, and the new features ran on test servers with scripted pilots. Update the game, or get it from the [download page](https://spacecorps.github.io/play/) or Hangar.

**Forge Sockets**
- **Tier-ups never fail.** Awakened, Resonant, Radiant and Ascendant replace Tainted, Godly, Rupturing and Eternal (the Eternal rarity stays). Each step wakes a socket (Awakened has 1, Ascendant 4) and costs 10,000 to 833,000 Credits plus materials; the last also 2,667 Thulium.
- **Shards fill the sockets.** A step gives no buff itself. An **Echo Shard** tunes a socket (150 for all four), a **Lattice Shard** re-lays a tuned one, a rare **Heartshard** lets you pick any free stat. They drop from solo gates, clan dungeons, some aliens and rare leaders; a wipe takes loose ones unless they are in the Transport Cache.
- **Gear wears down.** Integrity runs 0 to 100. Crafted and looted gear starts at a random 70 to 100; each started 12 points under 100 is a fracture (2 points off a buff). A re-lay costs 4 to 6 integrity, a merge gives the pieces' average minus 6, and a Repair gives back 12 for Lattice Shards.
- By our model (not played), the Echo Shards for one fully tuned piece take about 11 hours with a clan, 51 without.

**The Miyuki**
- **A support ship in five levels.** Research it after the Paragon, make level I, upgrade it in place to V (6,000 Thulium for I, 75,000 for V).
- **Channel Heal (from level II) and Channel Shield (from level III)** link you for 5 seconds to a group or company mate within 1,200 units. Heal gives them 50,000 hull a second (75,000 at IV and V), Shield 75,000 shield a second. These numbers are unplayed.

**Missions**
- **44 new missions for levels 11 to 14:** ten regular missions and one Special per level. Kill counts and time limits are estimates.
- **The Gate Line:** eight more missions on their own Mission Control tab, 3 active at a time, opening at pilot levels 12 to 14.
- A hidden series of missions is in the game for pilots who have come a little way. We say no more.

**Solo gates on Singapore and US East**
- Build at home, launch to a regional server and jump through the portal: the run is played in an arena there (two per world, an unmeasured default; if both are busy you wait). Clan dungeons (now 3 pilots, not 4), Wardens, the Forge and the Skylab stay at Home.

**Base city, Commerce, Healing**
- The Mission Control station in every company sector is now a small space city, and a post stands in the x-4 sectors (M-4, T-4, G-4). **That post has no safe zone: you can be attacked there.**
- **Commerce** now also lowers craft and Forge prices, by up to 15 % at level 100. **Healing**, a new Pilot Skill, runs x1.00 at level 1 to x1.30 at level 100 for your Repair Drone, Emergency Repair and Heal Pod (first values).

**Weaker or costlier**
- Rocket Launcher III to V and Laser Optics II to V use up the piece of the tier below, taken when you queue, so while a V is made from your IV you have no launcher above a III.
- A Chroma Archive gives one skin for one ship, not a collection: about 214 openings at the median to fill the pool (was 120).
- The Ironclad, Storm and Wraith need the Paragon first (owners keep theirs). The Wraith has 13 laser slots, not 14.
- You can accept or claim missions only within 1,600 units of a Mission Control station.
- Honor counts for PvE ranking points (100 Honor is 1 point); ranks shift at the first server restart.
- The Archivist achievement asks for all 209 technologies (187 before).

**Servers**
- The server and the game go live together on Helsinki, Singapore and US East. A game from before 0.5.7 cannot use the Forge and is asked to update. Nothing is moved, paid or removed.
- The Android and iOS apps are not part of this release.
