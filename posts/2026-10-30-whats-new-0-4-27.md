---
title: 0.4.27: what's new
date: 2026-10-30
---
**New**
- **Repair ships:** two at every company base heal your company's pilots by **250 hull a second each** within 2,000 units. Enemies can shoot them down; they are back after 2 minutes.
- **Every docked Mole mines** (up to five), each on its own rock with its own beam.
- **Titles over names** on the map, small and dim in the title's colour. In a crowd only names show.
- **Boosters** now count the clan's and the P.E.T.s' boosts in each category's total.

**Changed**
- **Rockets:** every blast is **50 % wider**; the N.U.K.E. reaches **1,350** units. Damage is the same.
- **Skylab:** the Ammo Printer and the Rocket Factory make **twice as much**. The Trophies Bay counts loot only, so Credits and Thulium take no room and the Bay fills far more slowly.
- The **Ironclad** costs **100** Reinforced Hull Plates (was 450).
- **Excavator Mining** enlarges the boxes of a run for everyone who picks them up.
- **Hive Breaker** and **Iron Tide** also drop a P.E.T. chunk.
- New pilots start with the **REP button** in hotbar slot 2, and the Production Queue timer no longer flickers.
- **Achievements:** the Credits and loot containers of achievements that 0.4.26 unlocked for veterans without paying them have now been paid out.

**To get it**
- **Update the game** to see the new screens. The update itself moves no Credits, Thulium or items.
