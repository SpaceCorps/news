---
title: 0.5.2 and 0.5.3: what's new
date: 2026-11-10
---

0.5.2 and 0.5.3 are live: rebalanced ships, a rockets rework with ten new special rockets, Clan Dungeons, servers in Singapore and US East with one Global chat, and a tour for new pilots. Update the game from Steam, Hangar or the [download page](https://spacecorps.github.io/play/).

**Ships and rockets**
- **Ships rebalanced:** new hull, speed and laser numbers for every base ship, an **auxiliary slot** on all of them and a **tenth hull plate slot** for the Wraith. The 13 designs follow their ships.
- **Rockets rework:** only Tier I is sold, for **1,000 Credits**, and the tiers moved up. **Rocket Launcher II to V** fire 2 to 5 rockets a press.
- **Ten new special rockets:** Freeze, EMP, Shield Breaker, Toxic, Marking, Cooldown, Flares, Ricochet, Support and the **Gravitational N.U.K.E.**
- **The Auto-Rocket CPU fires at asteroids too,** and the Tesseract's Facets fly twice as far from it.
- **Laser ammo is cheaper to craft:** a tenth of the Cataclysite and Quorvium.
- **Mining:** the eight rock-breaking missions pay less (Husk and Crystal **70,000** Credits, 90,000 before), three rocks of the first ring have more hit points and pay more, and every rock gives about a quarter less ore.

**Clan Dungeons**
- Clan Gates are now **Clan Dungeons**. The Warlord's room opens when **75 %** of the Crystalys and their Twins are down.
- Boosters drop from kills at stages 2 and 3. The Zone of Chaos follows the Warlord, its Rocket barrage hits four times as hard, and the Dark Matter card gives twice as much.

**More servers, one chat**
- **Singapore and US East** work like Helsinki: the hotbar and the rockets work, and **Claim Reward** works there (the reward is paid to your account at the home server).
- **Pick your server before you log in:** the rows on the start card are clickable, and the game remembers your pick.
- **Global chat is one chat for every server and world,** and the name card shows which server a pilot is on. Achievements are off on the regional servers.

**Worlds**
- **Auction lots for each world:** Alpha, Beta and Gamma each get their own hourly lots. The Market stays shared.
- A mission's hull limit is **1.5 times** as big in Beta and **2 times** as big in Gamma.

**Easier to learn, nicer to use**
- **A skippable tour for new pilots:** 11 steps in the station and 17 in your first flight. Replay it in Settings > General.
- **Esc closes windows,** Enter and Ctrl/Cmd+Enter answer dialogs, and the dialogs show their keys.
- A **Next step** card on the Dashboard, a **?** Windows guide on the flight toolbar, and a sidebar that lists every page, with a narrower icon rail. **Launch** is in the header of every page.
- **Davidski has a theme song:** "Gold Ironclad", an AI-generated rap track about the game's lore, plays around his ship when you fly within about 3,000 units of him. It fades with the distance.

**To get it**
- **Update the game.** The server and the game go live together, and the updates move no Credits, Thulium or items.
- A 0.5.2 game can still log in and fly, but it is not a supported pair. Cannot connect? Type https://play.spacecorps.xyz into the Server address field on the sign-in screen.
