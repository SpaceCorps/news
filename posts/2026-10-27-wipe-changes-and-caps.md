The Universe Wipe gets new rules from the first wipe after the update. The first one can cost you.

## The caps
At the wipe, **credits above 5 billion** and **Thulium above 10 million** are lost. What is below the caps is yours. The caps start with the first wipe, after that update, whose countdown begins on or after **12 November 2026, 00:00 UTC**, at least 14 days after this post. An earlier wipe runs without them.

## You keep
- All ships, drones, drone formations and P.E.T.s
- Technologies, Skylab progress, Wipe Point boosts, experience, honor, quests and skins
- Credits up to 5 billion, Thulium up to 10 million
- Everything in your Transport Cache
- Up to 1,000 of each combat rocket and 1,000,000 of each laser ammo type, even outside the Cache
- Unopened containers and special items

The Cache now holds one ship (locked until the wipe, it keeps all its equipment), 10 laser, 10 shield and 10 engine slots, and 100 tons of cargo. Everything else goes in by weight.

## What resets
- Your season rank (kept in your history) and the Wipe Point sources
- Alien kills count for the wipe you are in
- The Chrono-Gate parts and every other item outside the Cache
- Clans dissolve with their treasury
- Assembly crafts are cancelled and refunded, finished ones you did not collect too: collect them first

## What you get
- Wipe Points, Premium days, credits and Thulium for the rank you reached
- A look for the top ranks
- Loot containers and a season title

After the first wipe of the new rules, ranking points count only the experience, levels and kills you earn after the previous wipe. Honor does not set your rank.

Full rules: the wiki pages Wipe Timeline and Seasons and Rewards.
